Privacy Policy

Applies to: Watch Later Cleaner browser extension · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Watch Later Cleaner does not collect, sell or share your browsing data. It only works on your YouTube Watch Later page, and only when you start it.

What the extension stores

On your own device, using the browser's extension storage:

  • Your settings (speed, auto-refresh).
  • How many videos the extension has removed in total, used for the free limit.
  • Whether you have unlocked the paid version.
  • Temporary progress of a running clear, so it can resume after a page reload.

This data stays in your browser and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

What the extension does on YouTube

When you click "Clear Watch Later", the extension opens your Watch Later playlist and clicks YouTube's own "Remove from Watch later" menu option for each video, just like you would by hand. It does not read, record or send your watch history, account details, or any other YouTube data.

Payments

The paid unlock is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. You enter your payment details on Stripe's secure checkout page; the extension and Tidyware Labs never see or store your card details. ExtensionPay stores your email address and payment status so you can restore your purchase on another device. See their privacy policies: ExtensionPay, Stripe.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, other than ExtensionPay and Stripe for processing a purchase you choose to make. We do not use data for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to the extension.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. For purchase records, contact us and we will handle the request together with ExtensionPay.
